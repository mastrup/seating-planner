WHO SITS TOGETHER

Linked groups

Everyone in a group must sit together. Use the pencil on a group card to give it a name. Add a person to a group, or link two unlinked people to start one. Adding someone already linked merges their whole group. The group's table is used when seated; otherwise a seated member's table is used. Removing a member leaves their seat unchanged.

Drag a person onto another card to move just that person into it. Dropping onto an unlinked person's card creates a group. They take the destination person's seating location, including Unseated. On touch screens, drag using the :: handle. The dropdown controls still merge whole groups.

Drop a person here to unlink them without changing their seat. Or use their Remove button.
